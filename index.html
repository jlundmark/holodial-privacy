HoloDial privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

HoloDial is a watch face for Wear OS watches with a phone app that sets it up. This policy covers both. HoloDial has no accounts, no adverts and no analytics, and its developer does not receive any of your data unless you email it.

Location

HoloDial uses your phone's location for one feature: changing the watch face theme when you arrive at or leave a place you have saved, such as home or work.

  • This only happens if you save a place and allow location access.
  • If you choose "Allow all the time", HoloDial collects location data in the background, including when the app is closed or not in use, so that the theme can change without you opening the app.
  • Your location is checked on your phone and compared with your saved places. With a watch that has the face sent to it, this is done about every 15 minutes, because Android's own reports of arriving and leaving can be late. It is never sent to the developer, and no location history is kept.
  • The saved places themselves (name, position and size) are stored on your phone and sent to your own watch over the Wear OS link between the two.

You can stop this at any time by deleting your saved places or by turning off location access for HoloDial in Android's settings.

Addresses

HoloDial uses address lookup services in two cases:

  • When you type an address to find a place, the text you type is sent so that matches can be suggested, together with the rough position (to about 10 km) of one of your saved places so that nearby results come first.
  • When a saved place is shown, its position is sent so that it can be displayed as a street address. If you save a place with "Use my current location", this is your position at that moment.

The services are Android's built-in address lookup, provided by Google, and for typed searches also Photon, run by Komoot (photon.komoot.io). They handle these requests under their own privacy policies. The developer of HoloDial does not receive them.

Icon packs

If you choose to download an icon pack, your phone fetches it from a public file service (jsDelivr). As with any download, that service sees your phone's internet address. Nothing about you or your settings is sent. A picture you turn into an icon is handled like a photo, as described below.

Photos

If you choose a photo as a watch face background, HoloDial makes a small copy, stores it on your phone and sends it to your own watch. Your photos are not sent anywhere else unless you share a theme that uses one.

Settings

Your themes, colours, icons, labels and other settings are stored on your phone and your watch and synced between the two. They may be included in Android's own device backup if you have that switched on, along with your saved places, your photo backgrounds and the icons you made.

Sunrise and sunset times are worked out from your saved places and your time zone. Steps and heart rate on the face are read and shown by the watch itself; HoloDial does not receive them.

Theme files

If you share a theme, HoloDial makes a file holding that theme: its colours, text, the names of its icons, the picture of any icon you made from your own image and, if it has one, its photo background. The file goes only where you send it with Android's share sheet. Nothing else about you or your settings is in it. A theme file you import is read on your phone.

Feedback

If you use "Send feedback", your own email app opens with a message to the developer that lists the app version, your phone model and Android version, and your watch's name. You can read and change the message before sending it, and it is only sent if you send it.

Sharing

HoloDial does not sell your data, and the developer never receives it unless you send it in an email. The only data that leaves your devices without you sending it yourself is what goes to the address lookup services described above and what Android's own backup keeps in your Google account if you have that switched on; downloading an icon pack sends nothing but the request for its files. Theme files and feedback emails go only where you send them.

Children

HoloDial is not directed at children under 13.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling HoloDial from your phone and watch removes everything it stored. The developer holds nothing to delete. A device backup in your own Google account is managed in Android's settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address with a new date.
